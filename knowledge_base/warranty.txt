NovaTech Electronics
====================

Warranty Policy

Standard Warranty

All eligible NovaTech products include a 2-year limited manufacturer's warranty.

Nova+ Warranty

Nova+ members receive an additional one year of warranty coverage, for a total warranty period of 3 years.

Warranty Covers

The warranty covers:
- Manufacturing defects
- Hardware failures during normal use
- Factory-installed components

Warranty Does Not Cover

The warranty does not cover:
- Accidental damage
- Water or liquid damage
- Fire damage
- Theft or loss
- Cosmetic scratches
- Damage caused by unauthorized repairs
- Normal wear and tear

Warranty Claims

Customers should contact customer support with:
- Order number
- Description of the issue
- Photos or videos if applicable

Repair or Replacement

NovaTech will determine whether a product will be:
- Repaired
- Replaced
- Refunded

The decision depends on product condition, repair feasibility, and warranty eligibility.
