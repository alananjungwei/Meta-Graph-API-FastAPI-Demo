Returns and Refund Policy

Return Window

Customers may return eligible products within 30 calendar days of delivery.

Return Conditions

Returned products must:
- Be in good condition
- Include all original accessories
- Include the original packaging whenever possible

Customers may inspect and test products in a manner similar to what would be possible in a physical retail store.

Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned:
- Customized products
- Gift cards
- Products intentionally damaged by customers

Return Shipping

Customers are responsible for return shipping costs unless:
- The product arrived damaged.
- The incorrect item was shipped.
- The customer is a Nova+ member.

Refunds

After the returned product passes inspection, refunds are issued to the original payment method.

Refund processing typically takes up to 5 business days.

Damaged Products

If a product arrives damaged during shipping, customers should contact customer support within 48 hours of delivery and include photos of the damage.

Order Cancellation

Orders may be cancelled before they are shipped.

Once an order has been dispatched, customers must follow the standard return procedure.
