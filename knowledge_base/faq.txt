NovaTech Electronics
====================

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do you ship outside the European Union?

A:
No. NovaTech currently ships only within Germany and other European Union member states.


Q: Is shipping free?

A:
Standard shipping is free for orders over €50. Nova+ members receive free standard shipping on every order.


Q: How can I track my order?

A:
Customers receive a tracking number by email once the order has been shipped.


Q: Can I cancel my order?

A:
Yes, provided the order has not yet been shipped.


Q: How long do refunds take?

A:
Refunds are typically processed within 5 business days after the returned product has been inspected.


Q: How long is the warranty?

A:
Most products include a 2-year limited warranty. Nova+ members receive a total warranty period of 3 years.


Q: Do you offer express shipping?

A:
Yes. Express shipping is available for Germany and European Union destinations.


Q: Can I return a product I opened?

A:
Yes. Customers may inspect and test products as they would in a physical retail store. Products must still meet the return eligibility requirements.


Q: What payment methods do you accept?

A:
NovaTech accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Klarna.


Q: How do I contact customer support?

A:
Customers can contact NovaTech through Facebook Messenger, email support, live chat, or the contact form on the website.
